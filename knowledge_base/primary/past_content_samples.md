Link: https://medium.com/@np8350/the-illusion-of-control-in-ai-interfaces-013611741457

## Post 4: "When the Interface Loses Its Human"
Link: https://medium.com/@np8350/when-the-interface-loses-its-human-2bd843111e4e
Theme: AI agents are changing software interfaces, but the real design problem isn't making the machine legible. It's preserving human authority inside a system that increasingly acts on its own.

## Voice Pattern Across All Four

- Opens with a concrete scenario or claim, not a definition or a "AI is transforming X" statement
- Short declarative sentences, often one idea per sentence
- Poses a real question partway through rather than only asserting conclusions
- Recurring thread: control, authority, and oversight in agentic AI systems, connecting back to the book's core argument that the interface hides the real system underneath

## Why This Matters for Content and Consulting Work

New content should match this thread and this rhythm, not introduce a different voice or a different core concern. If a generated piece could have been written about any AI product by any AI commentator, it's off. These four posts are all recognizably about the same underlying question: who or what is actually in control, and does the interface tell you the truth about that.
