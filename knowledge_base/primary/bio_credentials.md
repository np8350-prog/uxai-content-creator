## What I actually do

I diagnose why AI products fail before they fail, using a six-pattern framework I built from real client work and documented in my book, "The Interface Is Not the System." I don't start with design or engineering. I start with whether the use case is even sound.

## Author

Author of "The Interface Is Not the System," which documents six recurring failure patterns in AI product development: Solving the Wrong Problem, Waiting for Perfect Data, Forgetting the Humans, Underestimating Total Cost of Ownership, AI Literacy Gaps at the Top, and No Clear Path Beyond Proof of Concept.

## Why This Matters for Content and Consulting Work

Content should draw on real experience, not credentials as decoration. The MIT certificate and CS background matter because they let me ask engineers the right questions, not because they're a badge. Lead with what the credential lets me actually do for a client, not the credential itself.
